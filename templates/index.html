Hi, I'm Arnaldo.

I'm a creative who learned to code because doing things the slow way got boring. I work as a freelance AI creative producer and consultant, helping teams put generative AI to work on real creative output. I'm also AI Creative Producer at Kleinanzeigen, where creative work meets generative models.

Before that I spent five years at idealo, building Magpie, a system that turns product data into social creatives and AI video across European markets, and co-building ALIX, a compliance assistant that took first place at idealo's AI Innovation Weeks.

And before that, ten years running Barbaroffa: 15 million organic views for an Italian audience, made by a small team of volunteers with no budget.

Italian by birth, Berliner by choice. I speak Italian, English, German, French, Spanish and Mandarin, which mostly means I can fail to be funny in six languages.
