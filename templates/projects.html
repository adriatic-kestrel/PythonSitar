Barbaroffa was my independent media lab, spread across YouTube (77K subscribers), Instagram (20K) and TikTok (10K).

It started as a gaming channel and grew into current affairs commentary, international communication and language content for Italian audiences. Along the way I tested formats, adapted them across platforms and built a community without spending on ads.

It paid for itself through Amazon affiliate links, brand collaborations, consulting and Udemy courses.
